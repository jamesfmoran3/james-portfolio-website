# Brazilian Peppertree Monitoring in Florida

**Goal** — Turn ecological monitoring records into maps that can support biological control program tracking and communication.

## What I did

- Cleaned and organized field monitoring records for spatial analysis.
- Mapped site visits, thrips detections, and recorded damage and establishment observations.
- Summarized thrips releases by county to show the geographic distribution of releases across Florida.

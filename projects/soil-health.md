# Soil Health and Sustainable Agriculture

**Research context** — I supported USDA Sustainable Agriculture Research and Education (SARE) work involving soil health, crop productivity, compost, and beneficial soil microorganisms.

## What I contributed

- Collected soil and compost samples for laboratory analysis.
- Assisted with field preparation, drip irrigation installation, seed propagation, and plot maintenance.
- Helped maintain research plots through irrigation, weed management, and pest monitoring.
- Presented research concepts to Florida farmers and agricultural stakeholders.

This work connected field research with grower outreach and gave me experience supporting sustainable agriculture research from sample collection through communication.

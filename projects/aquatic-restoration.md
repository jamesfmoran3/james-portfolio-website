# Invasive Aquatic Plants and Eutrophication

**Question** — How can biological control fit into restoration strategies for freshwater systems affected by invasive plants and excess nutrients?

## What I did

I reviewed scientific literature, extension publications, and invasive species profiles. I compared case studies involving hydrilla, water lettuce, and water hyacinth, focusing on the ecological effects of dense plant growth and the benefits and limitations of biological control.

## Main takeaway

Nutrient enrichment can favor invasive aquatic plants, while dense growth and decomposition can worsen water quality. My presentation examined how invasive plant management can be combined with nutrient reduction, native plant restoration, and long-term monitoring.
